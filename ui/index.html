03 — Monitor

Keep watching

Every event, a document upload or a monthly bulletin update, follows the same five steps. Nothing reaches you unless it clears the gate.

  1. Step 01Read the documentsEach page is read for its governing date and the exact text it came from. Live Bedrock when it is available, a verified recorded response otherwise, and the label always says which.
  2. Step 02Compare the governing datesThe I-94 admit-until date against the I-797 validity end date. Arithmetic, not judgment.
  3. Step 03Apply the deterministic decision gateMateriality, actionability, window, novelty. Plain Python, and the same gate for every event.
  4. Step 04Prepare the attorney-review actionA draft whose dates and flag are fixed text the model cannot edit. Approval writes a receipt on this clock, event, and rule version.
  5. Step 05Keep watchingBulletin checks can run unattended on a schedule. A repeat stays silent; only a change that clears the gate is surfaced, once.
