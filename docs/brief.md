# Agents for Humans (AWS) · the brief, the rules, the dates

Source: https://agentsforhumans.devpost.com/ and /rules (read 2026-09-08).

## What to build
"Build a new AI agent with Strands Agents SDK that does real work for people. Your agent should take on something people actually deal with and handle it end to end."

Tracks (pick one):
- **Everyday Agents**: daily life, home, money, health, errands, family. "The best ones run quietly in the background and only ping you when there's a real decision to make."
- **Professional Agents**: makes someone dramatically better at work they already do. "Target the repetitive, judgment-heavy tasks that eat their day."
- **Good Neighbor Agents**: helps groups: neighborhoods, nonprofits, food banks, schools, libraries, small local orgs.

AgentCore deployment: recommended, strengthens Technical Implementation, not required.

## What to submit
- Text description: what it does, who it's for, how it works
- PUBLIC repo URL with all source, assets, setup instructions
- MIT or Apache license visible in the repo's About section
- README
- Architecture diagram
- Demo video, max 5 minutes: working demo + pitch covering (1) the problem, (2) who it's for, (3) why it matters. Slides, screen recording, voiceover fine; no camera needed.
- AWS Builder ID
- Optional: live demo link (scores higher on Technical Implementation)
- Bonus: builder.aws.com posts with "Agents for Humans" in the title, 0.2 each, up to 3 (max +0.6 on a 5-point scale). Write all three.

## Rules that matter
- **Newly created during the Submission Period (Aug 10 to Sep 14, 2026).** Standard tools, libraries, templates, AI coding assistants are fine. "Must disclose any other pre-existing code or work incorporated into the Project."
- Team: no size cap. All members register on Devpost. One Representative submits and splits any prize.
- One person may join more than one team and also enter solo. Multiple submissions allowed if "unique and substantially different."
- Eligibility: age of majority; residency exclusions (Russia, Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, Crimea, DNR/LNR, Belarus, UAE, Quebec, and a list of APAC/LatAm countries incl. Australia, Brazil, Argentina, Italy, Singapore, Malaysia, Philippines, Thailand, Vietnam, Indonesia, Hong Kong). US and India residents are fine. No visa-status exclusion. Amazon employees and households excluded.
- IP: entrants own it. Sponsor gets a license to judge and to promote for three years.
- Judging: five equal criteria: Technical Implementation, Design, Potential Impact, Creativity & Originality, Presentation.
