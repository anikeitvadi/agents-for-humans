Agents for Humans Draft Board

Thirty workflows people still do by hand in 2026, dug up by five research passes (everyday admin, logistics, community, small business, professional), scored by the researchers, then re-ranked for what two people can ship in four days and put in a five-minute video. Scores are U·D·A·I·E: uniqueness, demoability, data availability, impact, end-to-end fit, each out of 5.

Recommended build

One engine that reads the government so you don't have to. Two modules: immigration clocks and product recalls.

Both are Everyday track, both run quietly and ping only on a real decision, both have verified public feeds (USCIS case status, Federal Register, the visa bulletin; CPSC, NHTSA, FDA recalls). Same spine: watch official sources, match them to your documents and receipts, act, ping. Immigration is the flagship and the story only we can tell. Recalls are the universal one and the cleanest live demo on the board. If we go Professional instead, #5 DataQs is the strongest technical demo: type any real USDOT number and watch it work on live data.

1

Immigration status guardian

Everyday
1.26M people in the employment-based green-card backlog (997K Indian-born), 1M+ international students, every H-1B, H-4, OPT, DACA, and TPS holder. Their lives run on clocks the government moves.
The moment: three PDFs in. "You have 55 days of status, not 20 months": CBP cut the I-94 to the passport expiry, and 180 days over is a 3-year bar. Attorney email drafted. Then it reads the visa bulletin unattended and says "file the I-485 this month."
U5 D4 A3 I4 E5 · 21USCIS case status API verified (sandbox 1,000/day, prod 150K/day)Federal Register API public
I-94 has no API, so that part is document parsing. "Not an every-day use case" is true, but the brief rewards every-day running. Widen it with DMV, passport, and benefit-renewal clocks and it's everyone.
2 people, 4 days: yes

Recall-to-remedy

Everyday
Anyone who owns things. Consumer recall participation is about 6% (4% under $20). About 1 in 3 recalled cars is never repaired, roughly 149.5M vehicles.
The moment: a CPSC recall drops during the demo. The agent matches it to a 2024 receipt in the inbox, prefills the remedy form, and pings: "refund $218 or voucher $350?"
U4 D5 A5 I4 E5 · 23CPSC, NHTSA, openFDA all live JSON, no auth
Shares a spine with #1 (watch a feed, match to your life, act, ping) and with class-action claims. Cleanest live demo on the board.
2 people, 4 days: yes

Tier 3 · Real, but crowded or thin

The researchers found the pain but also found products already on it, or evidence too thin to bet a week on.

  • Medical bill audit and EOB reconciliation 19% of in-network claims denied, under 1% appealed. Uniqueness 2: HealthLock, Goodbill, Counterforce all exist. The only open gap is reconciling against the hospital's own posted rate.
  • Class-action claims matched to your purchases Median claim rate 9%. Payout and ClaimDepot list settlements; none match receipts. Bot-fraud crackdown makes the attestation step the whole product.
  • School immunization compliance 3·4·4·4·4. Photograph a handwritten Spanish vaccine card, run the CDC evaluation logic, draft the parent letter. Real, but school nurses aren't buying at a hackathon.
  • Pharmacy below-cost claim appeals Strong data (daily remittances, published cost benchmarks), uniqueness 2.5.
  • Certified payroll for small subs 15 to 25 hours a week at five projects, but LCPtracker, Elation, Points North, and Miter own it. Uniqueness 2.
  • Contractor prequalification questionnaires 40 to 80 hours the first time, only consultants do it, uniqueness 4, but the portals are gated so the demo is against an export.
  • OEM warranty claims from a nameplate photo 30 to 60 day windows, $50 to $75 fees per claim. Score 17.
  • Tenant-union building dossier Three to five government sites per building, no national database. JustFix does it for NYC only. Score 17.
  • Neighborhood agenda watch and public comment Alerts already exist (Legistar-based tools); the open gap is drafting and filing the comment. Uniqueness 2.
  • Cross-border returns duty recovery Duty often exceeds product value. Data 2, end-to-end 2. Score 14.

Already owned by a product, cut by the research: Amazon FBA reimbursements (Getida, Carbon6), duty drawback (Zollback), dock scheduling (Opendock shipped an MCP server), restaurant supplier ordering (Choco), farm CSA routing, oversize permits, machine-shop quoting, pet health certificates (Alita, GlobalVetLink), plan-check comment responses (Sumeria, TrussNote), IRS notice responses (TaxNotice.ai), youth-sports compliance (Ankored), flight-delay compensation (AirHelp, and DOT refunds are now automatic).

From our chat, not researched yet

Ideas we raised before the research ran. No evidence pass on these; scores would be guesses.

  • Doctor appointment getter that beats ghost networks Reads the insurance card, finds providers, actually calls offices to verify plan and new-patient status, books the earliest slot, prefills intake. The directory-error problem is well documented; the voice loop is the build risk. Worth a research pass if health is where we land.
  • Storm dispatch swarm for a neighborhood Intake, planner, dispatcher, monitor agents running evacuation rides and supplies on live feeds and a map of Houston. Coolest video, biggest build, no evidence pass.
  • Delivery-platform reconciliation for restaurants Pull DoorDash, Uber Eats, and Grubhub statements, reconcile against the POS, dispute wrongful refunds and fee errors. The consumer version (deals and macros) is dead: no ordering API, terms forbid automation.
  • Supplements and peptides Dead. Buying recommendations aren't "real work," and peptides are a liability AWS judges won't reward. The only defensible piece is an interaction check against real prescriptions.

How this was made

Five research passes ran in parallel on Sep 8, one per lane, each screening 12 to 14 candidates and writing up its top six with steps, hours, data sources, existing tools, and evidence links. Reddit was unreachable from the research environment, so evidence comes from trade forums, industry surveys, court filings, government data, and vendor case studies (marked as such in the full reports). Every "verified live" note means the researcher hit the API that day.

The tiers and order on this page are a judgment layer on top of the researchers' scores: how a five-minute video lands, whether two people can finish by Sunday, and which stories we can tell honestly. "Found by 2 lanes" means two researchers surfaced the same workflow without seeing each other's work, which is the strongest signal a gap is real.
