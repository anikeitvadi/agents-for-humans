The problemTwo agencies, two dates, one that counts

The approval notice in your drawer says twenty months.

The record that governs your stay says fifty-five days.

CBP stamps an admit-until date on your I-94 when you land. USCIS prints a different date on your I-797. If your passport expires first, CBP cuts the I-94 to the passport, and nothing tells you.

"The 'Admit Until Date' is the date that the traveler's immigration status expires in the U.S." · CBP, I-94 Expiration Dates fact sheet, Pub. 0326-0715

    How it's builtThe model works at three edges, the decision lives in code
    Edge 1 · Read

    Extraction with evidence

    Amazon Bedrock Converse reads each document. Every field returns with the exact text it was read from and a status: extracted, missing, or ambiguous. Anything unsure blocks the rule; no arithmetic runs on a guess.

    agent/llm/extract.py

    Edge 2 · Draft

    An intro around a fixed core

    A Strands Agent writes only the opening of the attorney message. The dates and the flag are fixed text it cannot edit; if the model is absent or fails, the core ships alone.

    agent/llm/draft.py

    Edge 3 · Ask

    A guardian with five tools

    A Strands Agent whose only tools are the engine's checks, on the same ledger the UI uses. It may call a tool and report the result. It never computes dates or decides status itself.

    agent/llm/guardian.py

    Between the edges: a deterministic engine. Schema, rules, decision gate, ledger. Plain Python, 204 offline tests, five live ones.
